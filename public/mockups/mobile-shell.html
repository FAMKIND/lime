Lime on a phone: the proposed shell (a mock, for a yes or no)

Five pictures at the real phone size (390 × 844), made from Lime’s own styles, avatars, rows and toast. Nothing here is connected to the app. Tell me what to change: the dock items, floating or classic, the top bar, the sheet.

What will be native in the build

  • Share uses navigator.share (the phone’s own share sheet), falling back to copying the link.
  • The microphone and timezone pickers become a native <select>.
  • Keyboards follow the field: type=email / inputmode for email, phone and search.
  • The composer’s return key is labelled Send with enterkeyhint="send".
  • Files and photos use the native file and photo pickers (<input type=file>).

What can’t be native on the web

  • Action menus can’t be real iOS or Android menus: they become bottom sheets like frame 3.
  • No haptics on iOS (Safari doesn’t offer them), so taps get no vibration there.
