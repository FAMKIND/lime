Need more help?

Is lime free?
Yes, always. No ads and no fees. lime is funded by donations and grants.
Can lime read my messages?
No. Messages, calls and files are end-to-end encrypted, so only the people in the chat can read them. Read the privacy policy.
Who can join?
Teachers, 18 or older. Students don't have accounts.
Does it work without internet?
Nearby phones can pass sealed messages along by Bluetooth when there's no connection. They can't read what they carry, and it's a switch you control.
How does lime keep students safe?
Only teachers can use lime. Before you post publicly, lime asks you to check that no student can be identified. Anyone can report something, and moderators act on reports. See the community guidelines and safety page.
Is lime open source?
Yes, under the MIT licence. We'll link the code here when the repository is public.
Who's behind lime?
Teachers, and a nonprofit. Read our story.
