No signal? No problem.

Talk even without service.

Nearby phones can pass sealed messages along by Bluetooth, so a message can still get through on a field trip or in a school with poor coverage.

Carriers can't read what they pass on. It's a switch you control.

No internet. No bars. Your message still has a way home.
