# Demo pictures (LIME-286a)

Every picture below is from Pexels (free to use; credited anyway): objects, places and hands, no children, no identifiable faces. The people are initials on lime's eight colours, not photos. Originals: `~/Downloads/pexels-<author>-<id>.jpg`. Swap in your own pack by editing `demo.json` (see the README added in 286c).
