5. Revoke one device: **no path** (only `reset_account_keys`, which revokes all). Needs a `devices-revoke` function with a master-key proof; groups rotate on their own, jam tiers do not.

8. Sessions: refresh is single-use and shared (`AccountSession.swift:352`); any non-network refresh failure signs the device out (`:114`); local `[auth.sessions]` is off; **staging/production not checked**; **a fresh sign-in ended older sessions on staging** (cause not found).
