Safety and abuse

Draft, pending legal review.

To report abuse, use Report in the app, or email abuse@limemessenger.com. In an emergency, call local emergency services first.

Last updated: 1 November 2026 (draft).

Reporting abuse

Use Report in the app on a message, person, post, comment or page. Or email abuse@limemessenger.com. We aim to look at every report within 24 hours, and a child's safety at once.

What moderators can and cannot see

  • They see only what the reporter chose to send: the reason, a note, and the one item they included.
  • They cannot read anyone's end-to-end encrypted chats. Nobody at lime can.
  • They can act on public things: hide a post, take a page down, remove a public picture, or suspend an account.
  • The person reported is never told who reported.

Urgent cases

If someone is in danger, call your local emergency services first. Then tell us. lime is not an emergency service.

Law-enforcement requests

Send requests to legal@limemessenger.com [placeholder: to be confirmed]. Because lime is end-to-end encrypted, we can provide very little by design: an account's sign-in email and creation date, a user's device public keys, and what a report included. We cannot provide message content, contact lists or group membership. Where the law requires us to report something (for example child sexual abuse material to NCMEC), we do.
