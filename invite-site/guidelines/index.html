Community guidelines

Draft, pending legal review.

lime is a place for people who teach. Treat everyone with respect. Never share anything that puts a child at risk.

Last updated: 1 November 2026 (draft).

What is not allowed

  • Harassment or bullying, including repeated unwanted contact.
  • Hate or violence: attacking people for who they are, or threatening harm.
  • Sexual or graphic content.
  • Anything involving the abuse or sexualisation of a child. This is reported to the authorities.
  • Doxxing: sharing someone's private details without consent.
  • Students identifiable in your posts (faces, names, voices) without proper consent.
  • Spam, scams and fake offers.
  • Impersonation of a person, a school or lime.
  • Breaking the law, or helping others to.

What happens

Moderators look at every report, within 24 hours. A report about a child's safety goes to the top of the queue.

They can: dismiss the report, hide a post, take a page down, remove a public picture, or suspend an account. They tell the person what was done. The reporter is never named.

lime cannot see inside private chats. Moderators see only what you choose to send when you report.

How to report

Long-press a message, tap ⋯ on a profile, post, comment or page, and choose Report. Pick a reason, add a note if you like, and choose whether to include the item. You can also block someone at any time.

Urgent safety concern? Contact local emergency services first, then abuse@limemessenger.com.

How to appeal

If your content was removed or your account suspended and you think it was a mistake, write to abuse@limemessenger.com with your username. We will look again.
