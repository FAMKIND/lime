# Settings, profile and jam — Android differences (LIME-300)

The spec is the iOS app (`SettingsView.swift`, `ProfileScreens.swift` / `FieldEditorModel.swift`, `ProfileView.swift`, `JamPage.swift`); this page lists only where Android differs.

- **Settings** opens from your avatar on the link tab, as a full screen (the system back gesture closes it). Tonight it holds: the profile card; the four fields of your profile (Name, About, Username, School), each edited on its own screen with iOS's rules and words (name ≤ 40, About ≤ 140 counting the emoji as one, the seven presets, username rules, school ≤ 120); your masked email (read only); About lime (version, core version, Terms, Privacy, Guidelines, Safety); **Sign out** ("Your chats stay on this device, locked, until you sign in again.") and **Remove everything from this device** (the red one, with its own confirmation). Save is the one lime action on the editor.
- **Another person's profile** opens from a one-to-one chat's header (avatar and name): their name, username, school and About, read only (iOS's hero, links and actions are not built).
- **Jam** is the third tab: the posts of the jams you follow and your own, newest first, read only; a card is the byline (avatar, name or "name, by writer", "with …", date, "Edited"), the title and the first words, as one TalkBack stop that opens the post; the reader shows the title, byline and the post's Markdown (pictures later). The empty feed says "Subscribe to teachers from their profiles. Your posts show here too."
- **Not built yet:** profile photo and background, themes, privacy, notifications, linked devices, storage, work hours, calls settings, password change, account deletion; jam writing, comments, likes, saving, hiding, discovery, podcasts and video cards, bands.
