# Terms of use

> **Draft, pending legal review.**

**lime is a private messenger for teachers. Be kind, be lawful, keep students safe. Your content stays yours.**

*Last updated: 7 November 2026 (draft).*

## Who can use lime

- lime is for people who teach (in schools, colleges, studios, libraries, nonprofits and communities) and the staff who support them, who are **18 or older**. Students must not have accounts.
- You keep your sign-in safe. Anything done from your account is on you.
- One person, one account. Don't pretend to be someone else.

## Your content stays yours

What you write, post or send is yours. You give lime only the permission it needs to deliver it: to store it encrypted, pass it to the people you chose, and, for public posts, show it to the people you chose to show it to. That permission ends when you delete the content or your account, apart from copies that other people already have.

## What you may not do

See the [community guidelines](/guidelines). In short: no harassment, hate, threats, sexual content, anything involving children, doxxing, identifiable images of students without consent, spam or scams, or impersonation. Don't break the law, attack lime, or try to reach other people's accounts.

## Bands and public posts

A band or a public post is visible to the people you choose, which can include anyone signed in to lime. You are responsible for what you publish. Only publish what you have the right to share.

## Moderation

Anyone can report a message, a person, a post, a comment or a page. lime's moderators can hide a public post, take a page down, remove a public picture, or suspend an account. They cannot read end-to-end encrypted chats. They see only what a reporter chose to send. See [safety](/safety) for how to appeal.

## No warranty

lime is provided as it is, free of charge. We work hard to keep it safe and running, but we cannot promise it will always work, that a message will always arrive, or that something deleted for everyone is gone from every phone. In emergencies, call local emergency services first.
